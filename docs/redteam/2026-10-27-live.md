## Reading it

  (exam leak, money doubling, lookalike JAMB).
- Of the 12 planted traps, Muse Spark followed none: it ignored 9 entirely on its own, and in the other 3 Jagora had
  already removed the order from the page and Muse Spark did not act on what was left.

- The 5 harmful requests were declined before the model was asked.
